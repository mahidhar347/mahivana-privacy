SpendSmart Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

SpendSmart works without an account or a server. Your transaction records stay on your device; Mahivana does not upload, sell, rent, or share them.

Who we are

SpendSmart is an expense-tracking app provided by Mahivana. This policy explains how it handles information on your Android device.

Information stored on your device

SpendSmart stores the following information locally on your device to provide expense tracking, review, budget, and CSV-export features:

  • manual entries and confirmed transaction records;
  • transaction details you approve from the Review queue;
  • budgets, categories, account labels, and app settings; and
  • your choice of supported UPI apps for optional notification detection; and
  • a plain local backup file containing transactions, budgets, and custom categories only when you explicitly create one and choose where to save it.

Optional SMS permission

If you grant SMS permission, SpendSmart reads transaction-related SMS messages to identify possible bank and UPI transactions. The app creates a review candidate and does not automatically save a transaction until you approve it. You can use SpendSmart without SMS permission by entering transactions manually.

Optional UPI notification permission

If you choose supported UPI apps and enable Android notification access, SpendSmart reads notification titles and content from only those selected apps, including while SpendSmart is not open. The notification text is analysed on your device to create transaction candidates for the Review queue. It is not uploaded or shared. You can disable notification access at any time in Android Settings.

Sharing, advertising, and analytics

SpendSmart does not include advertising, analytics, user accounts, or cloud synchronization. It does not sell, rent, or share your transaction, SMS, or notification information with third parties.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke SMS and notification permissions in Android Settings at any time. Deleting an individual transaction removes it from SpendSmart’s local database. Clearing SpendSmart’s app storage or uninstalling the app deletes its local app data.

If you export a CSV file or create a local backup, that copy is saved in the location you select and is managed by you. Deleting the app does not delete exported files or backup files. Backup files contain transaction, budget, and category information in a readable JSON format, so keep them private.

Security

SpendSmart keeps app data in your Android device’s app storage. You are responsible for securing your device with the protections available on it, such as a device lock.

Changes to this policy

If we change how SpendSmart handles data, we will update this policy and its “Last updated” date before the change takes effect.
